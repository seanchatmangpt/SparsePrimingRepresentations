# SPR — Standing and the ALIVE Ladder

- Standing is a claim about evidence quality at a named boundary.
- `UNKNOWN` means the relevant fact has not been established.
- `UNSUPPORTED` means the requested capability or proof surface does not exist in the current system.
- `BLOCKED` means a required transition cannot currently proceed despite a known path.
- `BUILD_BROKEN` means the construction or validation surface fails before subject execution can be established.
- `PARTIAL_ALIVE` means meaningful execution was observed, but the full requested boundary was not proven.
- `ALIVE` requires observed execution against the exact admitted subject at the requested boundary.
- `REFUSED` is typed policy or authority rejection, not a synonym for failure.
- UNKNOWN is not admission.
- UNSUPPORTED is not refusal.
- A workflow definition is not a successful workflow run.
- Inspection is not execution.
- A checkpoint is not a crown; narrow success cannot silently inherit broader standing.
- Every standing claim should expose its subject identity, verifier, evidence, boundary, and falsifier.
