# SPR — Chatman Equation

- Action is manufactured only from admitted observation: `A = μ(O*)`.
- Raw observation `O` is partial, stale, noisy, or unauthorized until admission constructs `O*`.
- Admission aligns identity, scope, authority, evidence, constraints, and current state.
- `μ` is lawful manufacture, not arbitrary transformation.
- The artifact or action inherits standing only from admitted inputs and lawful manufacture.
- A receipt `R = receipt(A)` binds what happened to identity, authority, consequence, and replay.
- UNKNOWN is not admitted evidence.
- A plausible result without a receipt is not equivalent to an executed result.
- Inspection can inform admission but cannot substitute for execution.
- Fastest defensible local ALIVE is preferred over remote status theater.
- Every claim of success must name the admitted subject, observed execution, verification boundary, and falsifier.
- The equation compresses governance, execution, evidence, and replay into one manufacturing invariant.
