# SPR — Autonomic Software Manufacturing

- Software work can be modeled as manufacturing when specifications, transformations, verification, and receipts are explicit.
- The manufactured unit is a verified semantic change, not raw text generation.
- Deterministic generators convert admitted specifications into reproducible projections.
- Agents discover, select, construct, verify, and repair; they do not receive ambient authority to actuate.
- A manufacturing cell should know its candidate work, acceptance edges, dependencies, and fallback inventory before execution begins.
- Build failure is process information, not proof that useful production is impossible.
- When the product is learning and innovation, failed experiments can still manufacture durable knowledge if the learning is captured and reusable.
- Semantic commits are inventory units when each independently preserves meaningful knowledge or capability.
- Throughput without verification creates work-in-process debt.
- Verification without publication creates stranded inventory.
- Publication without receipts creates unverifiable history.
- The plant optimizes useful, independently meaningful, replayable change rather than token volume or activity theater.
